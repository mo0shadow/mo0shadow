## ✦ About Me

I'm an **IT graduate** passionate about building digital products where thoughtful design meets solid engineering.

My work sits at the intersection of **mobile development, product design, and business analysis**. I enjoy looking beyond the screen: understanding the problem, identifying user needs, exploring business goals, shaping the experience, and turning the result into a functional mobile application.

> 💫 Great products aren't built by code or visuals alone — they're built by understanding **why a product should exist, who it serves, and how it can make a real difference.**

- 📱 **Mobile Development:** Building cross-platform applications with Flutter and Dart.
- 🎨 **Product Design:** Exploring user flows, information architecture, wireframes, UI design, and interactive prototypes.
- 🔎 **Business Analysis:** Breaking down ideas, understanding requirements, defining features, and connecting user needs with business goals.
- 🧩 **Product Thinking:** Helping shape ideas from early discovery and problem definition to implementation-ready solutions.
- 🌱 **Always Learning:** Improving my technical skills, design process, and ability to build products that are both useful and maintainable.

I treat AI as a **teammate, not an autopilot**: I give it clear context, review everything it produces, and keep the final decisions human.
